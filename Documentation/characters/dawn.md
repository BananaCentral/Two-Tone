# Dawn Character Sheet

Battle-stage artwork uses the side-facing `assets/dawn-battle.png`; her HUD and party portrait use `assets/dawn-front.png`.

## Identity and base stats

Character ID: `dawn`. Dawn starts at Level 1 with base HP 50, Physical Attack 0, Magical Attack 40, Physical Defense 2, Magical Defense 6, and Speed 30. Stats use the shared character-level and loadout formulas.

## Passive: Resourceful

When Dawn is in the party, the party's Skill Point cap increases by 2 and battle starts with 5 Skill Points (up from the standard 3). With the standard cap of 5, Resourceful sets the cap to 7. The passive remains active if Dawn is defeated.

## Basic Attack

Deal Magical damage at a 2.0× multiplier and restore 10 Energy. A Basic Attack restores 1 party Skill Point under the shared default.

## Skill: Tune

Costs 1 party Skill Point, restores 20 Energy, and targets one living ally (Dawn may target herself). Choose the target by tapping their battlefield sprite or HUD portrait card. The target's next Basic Attack deals 40% more damage and restores 1 additional Skill Point when used. Tune lasts through the target's next scheduled turn, then expires. The enhanced-Basic effect is consumed by the first Basic during the duration; if the target does not use a Basic, that enhancement expires with Tune.

Dawn Duplicate 3 adds 20% Physical and Magical Attack and extends Tune to two target turns. Duplicate 4 adds a further 40% to the enhanced Basic's damage bonus, for +80% total.

While an ally is Tuned, Dawn's equipped signature weapon lets that ally ignore 20% of enemy Physical and Magical Defense. Premium weapon effects are active only while Dawn owns and has equipped the Premium weapon:

1. The Tuned ally takes 20% less damage.
2. The ally currently affected by Tune deals 20% more damage to enemy barriers for Tune's duration. This bonus applies only to that ally's attacks.
3. Applying Tune advances only its target by 20%.
4. The Tuned ally gains 20 percentage points of Critical Rate and 50 percentage points of Critical Damage.

The Tune status lasts for its stated number of target turns even after the enhanced Basic is used; the +40% Basic bonus itself triggers only once.

## Ultimate

Costs 100 Energy, targets one living ally (Dawn may target herself), restores energy equal to 50% of that ally's maximum Energy, advances their next turn by 100%, and restores 1 party Skill Point. Dawn Duplicate 1 restores 100% of the target's maximum Energy. Duplicate 2 restores 3 Skill Points instead of 1. Ultimates do not consume a scheduled turn.

## Character duplicates

1. Restore 100% of the Ultimate target's maximum Energy.
2. Restore 3 Skill Points with the Ultimate.
3. Tune grants +20% Physical and Magical Attack and lasts two target turns.
4. Tune's enhanced Basic bonus increases from +40% to +80%.
