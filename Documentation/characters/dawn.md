# Dawn Character Sheet

Dawn's standard weapon is **Radiant Pearl** and her premium weapon is **Radiant Diamond**. Her loadout, HUD, and party portrait use `assets/dawn-artwork.png`. Her battle pose follows her equipped weapon: `assets/dawn-radiant-pearl-battle.png` with Radiant Pearl and `assets/dawn-radiant-diamond-battle.png` with Radiant Diamond. The matching weapon artwork is `assets/dawn-radiant-pearl.png` and `assets/dawn-radiant-diamond.png`.

## Identity and base stats

Character ID: `dawn`. Dawn starts at Level 1 with base HP 50, Attack 40, Physical Defense 2, Magical Defense 4, and Speed 20. Stats use the shared character-level and loadout formulas.

## Passive: Resourceful

When Dawn is in the party, the party's Skill Point cap increases by 2 and battle starts with 5 Skill Points (up from the standard 3). With the standard cap of 5, Resourceful sets the cap to 7. The passive remains active if Dawn is defeated.

## Basic Attack

Deal single-target Magical damage at a 2.0× multiplier and restore 10 Energy. A Basic Attack restores 1 party Skill Point under the shared default.

## Skill: Tune

Costs 1 party Skill Point, restores 20 Energy, and targets one living ally (Dawn may target herself). The target's next Basic Attack deals 40% more damage and restores 1 additional Skill Point when used. Tune lasts through the target's next scheduled turn, then expires. The enhanced-Basic effect is consumed by the first Basic during the duration; if the target does not use a Basic, that enhancement expires with Tune.

Character Duplicate 2 gives the Tuned ally +20 percentage points of Critical Rate and +50 percentage points of Critical Damage. Duplicate 3 adds 20% Attack and extends Tune to two target turns. Duplicate 4 adds 40 percentage points to the enhanced Basic damage bonus, raising it from +40% to +80%.

While an ally is Tuned, Dawn's equipped signature weapon lets that ally ignore 20% of enemy Physical and Magical Defense. Premium weapon effects are active only while Dawn owns and has equipped the Premium weapon:

1. The Tuned ally takes 20% less damage.
2. The Tuned ally deals 20% more damage to enemy barriers for Tune's duration.
3. Ultimate restores 3 party Skill Points instead of 1.
4. Applying Tune advances its target by 50%.

Tune's damage, critical, defense-ignore, damage-reduction, barrier-damage, and action-advance bonuses remain active for Tune's duration. The enhanced Basic damage bonus and extra Skill Point trigger only on the first Basic Attack during Tune.

## Ultimate

Costs 100 Energy, targets one living ally (Dawn may target herself), restores energy equal to 50% of that ally's maximum Energy, advances their next turn by 100%, and restores 1 party Skill Point. Character Duplicate 1 restores 100% of the target's maximum Energy. With Premium Weapon Duplicate 3, the Ultimate restores 3 Skill Points instead. Ultimates do not consume a scheduled turn.

## Character duplicates

1. Restore 100% of the Ultimate target's maximum Energy.
2. Tune grants +20 percentage points of Critical Rate and +50 percentage points of Critical Damage.
3. Tune grants +20% Attack and lasts two target turns.
4. Tune's enhanced Basic bonus increases from +40% to +80%.
