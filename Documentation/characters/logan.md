## Kit

**Passive — Taste for Blood:** While Logan is on the field, Logan and allied units gain 10% Lifesteal. Character Duplicate 2 raises this bonus to 20%.

**Basic Attack —** Deals single-target Physical damage at a 1.0 multiplier, applies 1 Bleed stack, restores 10 Energy, and follows the standard 1 Skill Point basic-attack gain. Character Duplicate 1 doubles its multiplier to 2.0. Premium Weapon Duplicate 1 applies 2 Bleed stacks.

**Skill — Blood Shield:** Costs 1 Skill Point and consumes 25% of Logan's current HP. Logan and each living ally on the field receive a Shield equal to the HP consumed; this full amount is applied to each ally, not divided among them. It restores 20 Energy and Taunts each enemy for 25% over 2 turns. Character Duplicate 4 doubles the Shield applied to each ally. Premium Weapon Duplicate 3 changes the Taunt to 50% over 3 turns. Shields are additive and do not expire; see [Shield Standard](../shield-standard.md).

**Ultimate — Red Tide:** Costs 100 Energy and deals AoE Physical damage to all enemies at a 1.25 multiplier, applying 1 Bleed stack to each target. Character Duplicate 3 doubles the multiplier to 2.5. Premium Weapon Duplicate 2 applies 2 Bleed stacks and restores 1 Skill Point.

## Weapon Effects

- **Signature Weapon:** When an ally's Taste for Blood Lifesteal restores HP, Logan also heals by the amount actually restored to that ally.
- **Premium Weapon Duplicate 1:** Basic Attack applies 2 Bleed stacks instead of 1.
- **Premium Weapon Duplicate 2:** Ultimate applies 2 Bleed stacks instead of 1 and restores 1 Skill Point.
- **Premium Weapon Duplicate 3:** Blood Shield Taunt increases to 50% and lasts 3 turns.
- **Premium Weapon Duplicate 4:** Units with a Blood Shield deal 20% more Physical, Magical, and True damage while their Shield remains above zero. The bonus ends when their Shield is depleted.
