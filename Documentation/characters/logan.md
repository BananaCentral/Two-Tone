## Kit

**Passive — Taste for Blood:** While Logan is on the field, Logan and allied units gain 10% Lifesteal. Character Duplicate 4 raises this bonus to 20%.

**Basic Attack —** Deals single-target Physical damage at a 1.0 multiplier, applies 1 Bleed stack, restores 10 Energy, and follows the standard 1 Skill Point basic-attack gain. Character Duplicate 1 applies 2 Bleed stacks. Premium Weapon Duplicate 1 raises the multiplier to 2.0.

**Skill — Blood Shield:** Costs 1 Skill Point, consumes 25% of Logan's current HP, and converts the cost into an equal team Shield pool distributed as evenly as possible among Logan and living allies on the field. It restores 20 Energy and Taunts each enemy for 25% over 2 turns. Character Duplicate 2 doubles the Shield pool. Premium Weapon Duplicate 2 changes the Taunt to 50% over 3 turns. Shields are additive and do not expire; see [Shield Standard](../shield-standard.md).

**Ultimate — Red Tide:** Costs 100 Energy and deals AoE Magical damage at a 1.0 multiplier, applying 1 Bleed stack to each target. Character Duplicate 3 applies 2 stacks and restores 1 Skill Point. Premium Weapon Duplicate 3 raises the multiplier to 2.0.

## Weapon Effects

- **Signature Weapon:** When an ally's Taste for Blood Lifesteal restores HP, Logan also heals by the amount actually restored to that ally.
- **Premium Weapon Duplicate 4:** Units with a Blood Shield gain 20% Physical and Magical Attack while their Shield remains above zero. The bonus ends when their Shield is depleted.
