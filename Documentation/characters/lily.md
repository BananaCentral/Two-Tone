# Lily Character Sheet

## Identity and base stats

Character ID: `lily`. Lily starts at Level 1. Her base stats are HP 100, Physical Attack 0, Magical Attack 30, Physical Defense 4, Magical Defense 4, and Speed 30. Stats use the shared character-level and loadout formulas.

## Passive: Exposed

When Lily's Skill or Ultimate damages an enemy, that enemy receives Exposed. Exposed lowers both defenses by 10% of the target's applicable defense for two enemy turns. Lily Duplicate 2 allows two stacks and extends the duration to three enemy turns. Summon attacks apply Exposed as well. Exposed stacks multiply resistance reduction additively.

## Basic Attack

Deal Magical damage at a 1.0× multiplier and restore 10 Energy. It does not restore a Skill Point.

## Skill: Ricochet

Costs 1 party Skill Point, restores 20 Energy, and deals AoE Magical damage at 1.0×. Enemies hit receive a Ricochet mark for two enemy turns. When any ally or summon damages an enemy with Ricochet, every living Ricochet-marked enemy takes additional True damage equal to 10% of the triggering hit's damage. Each action instance can trigger a given mark once. Lily Duplicate 4 triggers twice per action; the second hit is half strength. Mark trigger history expires with the mark.

## Ultimate: Haunting

Costs 100 Energy and deals AoE Magical damage at 1.0×. It summons an independent Hauntling immediately after the attack. The Hauntling deals AoE True damage at 1.0× on its immediate first turn and its next two scheduled turns, then expires. Its damage applies Exposed and can trigger Ricochet. Lily Duplicate 1 also reduces damage dealt by hit enemies by 10% for their next turn. Lily Duplicate 3 doubles the Ultimate's initial damage and grants the Hauntling one additional turn.

## Character duplicates

1. Haunting applies a one-turn 10% damage reduction.
2. Exposed can stack twice and lasts three turns.
3. Haunting's initial damage is doubled; Hauntling lasts one additional turn.
4. Ricochet triggers twice per unique allied action; the second trigger deals 50% damage.

## Premium signature weapon

When a teammate or summon damages an Exposed enemy, Lily restores 5 Energy. Using Haunting restores 1 Skill Point.

1. Enemies entering the field receive Soul Mark and Ricochet for two turns.
2. Each Exposed stack increases damage dealt to barriers by 10%.
3. Each Exposed stack increases barrier-shatter damage by 5 percentage points.
4. Haunting advances each teammate by 20% and restores 20 Energy to every teammate, including Lily.

Premium duplicate effects map to Potential 2–5 and are active only while the owned Premium weapon is equipped. Soul Mark is a marker only; no additional Soul Mark effect is defined yet.
