# Daily Coin Reward Standard

## Claim Rules

- The Daily Reward grants 100 coins once per daily claim period.
- A claim period runs from 12:00 AM Eastern Time through the next 11:59 PM Eastern Time. The reset uses the `America/New_York` time zone, including daylight-saving changes.
- Players claim the reward manually from the **Daily Reward** tile on the main menu. The tile shows **Claim 100 Coins** when available and a claimed/reset state after use.
- The next claim becomes available at the next 12:00 AM Eastern reset. Missed claims do not accumulate.
- Weekly banner rotations remain on Monday at 12:00 AM Eastern, sharing the midnight reset time. See the [Featured Lottery Standard](store-standard/featured-lottery-standard.md).

## Saved State

- Coin balance and the last claimed period are saved separately for Regular Mode and Dev Mode.
- Save files include the last claimed period so loading a save preserves the claim state.
- The reward adds 100 coins to the player's current wallet and can be spent in the Store.
