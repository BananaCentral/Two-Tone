## Passive: Sharp Claws

Bear ignores 25% of both Physical and Magical Defense when calculating its damage.

## Basic Attack

- Targets every living ally.
- Deals True damage at a 1.0× multiplier to each target.

## Barrier and Battle Display

Bear uses the standard enemy Barrier equal to 20% of Max HP. Its transparent 256 × 280 battle sprite is `assets/bear.png`. The Boss HP is displayed at the top of the screen with one dot to the left of its HP bar.
