# Goblin

## Identity

- Category: Elite (`elite`)
- Runtime archetype: `goblin`
- Level: 1

## Base Stats

| Stat | Base value |
|---|---:|
| HP | 200 |
| Physical Attack | 40 |
| Magical Attack | 0 |
| Physical Defense | 12 |
| Magical Defense | 4 |
| Speed | 40 |

## Passive: Pursuit

After each Basic Attack, Goblin's next scheduled action advances by 20% of its turn interval.

## Basic Attack

- Chooses a random living ally as the primary target.
- Hits that ally and living allies in adjacent party formation slots, up to three targets total.
- Deals Physical damage at a 1.0× multiplier to each target.

## Barrier and Battle Display

Goblin uses the standard enemy Barrier equal to 20% of Max HP. Its transparent 256 × 512 battle sprite is `assets/goblin.png` and is used for battlefield rendering and its turn-order portrait.
