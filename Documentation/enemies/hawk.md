# Hawk

## Identity

- Category: Common (`common`)
- Runtime archetype: `hawk`
- Level: 1 in the current training encounter

## Base Stats

| Stat | Base value |
|---|---:|
| HP | 50 |
| Physical Attack | 10 |
| Magical Attack | 0 |
| Physical Defense | 2 |
| Magical Defense | 2 |
| Speed | 60 |

## Passive: Mach Speed

Mach Speed gives Hawk 20% Stealth. Stealth is hidden; it is not displayed as a primary stat. Incoming positive-damage attack hits roll that chance independently for Hawk, following the [Stealth Standard](../stealth-standard.md).

## Basic Attack

- Targets one random living ally.
- Deals Physical damage at a 1.0× multiplier.

## Barrier and Battle Display

Hawk uses the standard enemy Barrier equal to 20% of Max HP. Its transparent 256 × 256 battle sprite is `assets/hawk.png`; the sprite is used for battlefield rendering and its turn-order portrait.
